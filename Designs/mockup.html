How a card payment actually moves

Shopify Payments isn't available to UAE merchants, so checkout hands off to a third-party gateway for the payment step only. Everything before and after stays inside Shopify.
Step 1
Shopify Checkout
Customer completes contact, address, shipping.
→
Step 2
Clicks "Pay Now"
Custom payment app sends order total + currency (AED) to the gateway.
→
Step 3 — Leaves Shopify
Gateway Hosted Page
Card entry + 3D Secure happens here, not on Imperial's site.
→
Step 4
Bank Authorization
Issuing bank approves or declines via the card network.
→
Step 5 — Returns
Webhook Confirms Payment
Gateway notifies the app; order marked paid.
→
Step 6
Order Confirmation
Native confirmation page + email receipt.
Inside Shopify (native) Outside Shopify (gateway-hosted)

What's native vs. what's custom-built

Native to Shopify (no extra dev)
  • Contact, address, shipping steps
  • Order summary & totals calculation
  • Cash on Delivery
  • Order confirmation page & email
  • Abandoned checkout recovery emails
Custom development required
  • Connecting the chosen gateway
  • Handling redirect + return-to-Shopify
  • Listening for the payment webhook
  • Displaying BNPL/Apple Pay options
  • Reconciling settlement reports with orders
